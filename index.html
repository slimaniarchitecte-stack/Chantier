Relapse protocol

Dropped everything? Read this, then do one session.

  1. Missing is not relapsing. A relapse is a full week with nothing in it.
  2. Never restart. Re-enter at the same phase, at half volume, for one week. Then back to normal.
  3. The re-entry session is fixed. 20 minutes: squats, push-ups, plank, two rounds.
  4. Re-enter within seven days of noticing.
  5. Smallest thing first. Never the hardest session to make up for it.
  6. No audit. Do one session. The plan is unchanged.
  7. Two relapses in one phase means the plan is too big. Cut a session and keep it cut.

Behaviours

Fourteen rules. Dated ones lift on their own.

    Time sinks

    Where evenings actually go
    • Gaming with no end time.
    • PC tinkering that eats the whole evening.
    • Phone in the bedroom.
    • Scrolling architecture content instead of drawing it.
    • Watching training and football content instead of training.
    • Rebuilding the plan instead of running it.

    Do not cut carbohydrates.

    You are sprinting twice a week. Carbohydrate is the fuel for that. This is not a diet: eat enough, get the protein in, and remove the few things below.

    Eat

    The defaults
    • Eggs at breakfast, instead of bread and jam alone.
    • Half a rotisserie chicken with salad as the default lunch.
    • Grilled fish twice a week.
    • Tajine with chicken or kefta. Watch the bread volume.
    • Lentils, loubia and chickpeas at dinner.
    • Protein at roughly 1.6 g per kilo of body weight. enter kg
    • More food on gym and match days, not less.

    Cut

    Drinkable sugar goes first
    • FirstDrinkable sugar: sweetened mint tea, soda, packaged juice.
    • Msemen, harcha and sfenj as a daily breakfast.
    • Bread with every meal and as a utensil. Halve it, don't eliminate it.
    • The 16:00 café pastry.
    • Deep-fried street food as a regular lunch.
    • Energy drinks before training.
    • Heavy meals at 23:00.
